Our story · Los Angeles, 1962

Jewelry should mean something.

 

My dad was a gold leaf letterer at the St. Vincent’s Jewelry Center, on the corner of 7th and Hill in downtown Los Angeles.

One afternoon in the eighties, my sister Jen and I watched him sketch a necklace. A jeweler friend carved the wax, and the gold poured like lava. I was nine. We still wear those necklaces today.

“Jewelry is more than metal and stone. It is a work of art, and a wearable memory.”

That corner gave the brand its name, and it is still how I work. I listen to your story first, then I design around it.

Your jewellery arrives in our luxurious book packaging featuring velvet lining.
