Where our name comes from

The corner of 7th & Hill, downtown Los Angeles

My dad was a gold leaf letterer at the St. Vincent’s Jewelry Center, on the corner of 7th and Hill. I was nine when I watched gold poured into a necklace he had sketched. My sister and I still wear those necklaces today.

“Jewelry is more than metal and stone. It is a wearable memory.”
