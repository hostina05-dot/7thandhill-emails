Welcome to the 7H story

 

Your piece has had a few weeks to settle in. A few habits keep it beautiful for years.

Jewelry care

Last on, first off

Perfume, lotion and makeup go on before jewelry.

Clean gently

Warm soapy water, a soft brush behind the stones, rinse, pat dry. Pearls: wipe with a soft dry cloth, never soak.

Everyday wear

Solid gold, diamonds and gemstones are fine for daily washing and showers. Keep pearls dry.

Nothing harsh

No bleach, harsh chemicals, commercial jewelry cleaners or ultrasonic cleaners.

Store apart

Each piece on its own, in a soft pouch or lined box.
