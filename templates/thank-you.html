Jewelry care

Keeping it beautiful

Last on, first off

Put jewelry on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off first.

Keep it out of the pool

Chlorine and salt water are hard on stones and settings.

Store each piece apart

A soft pouch or its own place in the box keeps chains from tangling.

Clean gently

Warm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft cloth. Nothing harsh.
