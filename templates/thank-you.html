Keeping it beautiful

A few small habits keep jewelry luminous for years.

i.

Last on, first off

Put your jewelry on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off first at the end of the day.

ii.

Keep it dry

Remove it before swimming, showering or exercise.

iii.

Store it apart

Rest each piece on its own in a soft pouch, away from humidity and direct sun.

iv.

Polish gently

A soft, dry cloth is all it needs. Avoid harsh cleaners.
