A short guide

How to choose a piece that feels like you

Some people start with a charm. Some with a ring they never take off. There is no wrong place to begin, only a few cues worth listening to.

i

Start with the story

A date, a name, a person. The pieces we wear longest usually mean something.

ii

Choose for every day

Pick what you’d wear on an ordinary Tuesday. Solid gold is made for exactly that.

iii

Let it grow

Begin with one chain or one charm. A collection is built slowly, over years.

iv

Don’t worry about fit

If a ring doesn’t fit just right, we’ll resize it for free.
